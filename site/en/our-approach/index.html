Our approach

From the analysis of the need to the comparison of proposals.

The quality of any financing rests first on the quality of its preparation. Our path is the same for every transaction; its content depends entirely on yours.

  1. 01

    Understand the transaction

    The first stage is one of listening. We seek to understand what the transaction must achieve, within what timeframe, with which assets and under which constraints.

    • Nature of the need and objective pursued.
    • Assets concerned, holding structure and parties involved.
    • Timetable, deadlines and points requiring attention.
    • Exit options already identified or still to be built.
  2. 02

    Prepare the file

    A lender decides on concrete facts. We build a clear, quantified and documented file that anticipates questions and avoids needless back-and-forth.

    • Summary of the transaction and of its economic rationale.
    • Financial tables, assumptions and relevant sensitivities.
    • Possible security and available valuation material.
    • List of documents to gather with your advisers.
  3. 03

    Approach and compare

    We identify the lenders relevant to the transaction, present the file and compare the terms offered against your priorities, with no preconception as to the type of lender.

    • Selection of routes consistent with the transaction and its timetable.
    • Presentation of the file and answers to lenders' questions.
    • Comparative reading of proposals: cost, term, security, conditions.
    • Regular progress updates.
  4. 04

    Support through to completion

    Once a route is chosen, we follow the discussions through to their conclusion, in coordination with your legal, tax and accounting advisers.

    • Monitoring of conditions precedent and further requests.
    • Coordination with lawyers, notaries and accountants.
    • Attention to the timetable through to completion.
    • A shared debrief at the end of the transaction.

Coordination

We work with your advisers, not in their place.

Legal, tax and accounting advice remains with your usual advisers. Our role is to make the transaction intelligible to a lender and to lead the search for financing. This division of roles is clear from the first conversation.

A transaction to present to us?

A first conversation is enough to understand where the need sits. We will tell you plainly whether we can be useful.
