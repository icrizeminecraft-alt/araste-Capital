The value of advice

Beyond the rate.

A financing proposal is judged on five parameters: amount available, overall cost, security, repayment schedule and repayment terms. We organise the analysis of these parameters so that you can compare the options proposed.

Our method

From the analysis of the need to the comparison of proposals.

Four stages, the same for every transaction, from the first conversation to the financing being put in place.

  1. 01

    Understand the transaction

    A first conversation to establish the need, the assets, the holding structure and the timetable.

  2. 02

    Prepare the file

    A quantified, documented presentation that a lender can read, and the financing options worth considering.

  3. 03

    Approach and compare

    Approaching the relevant lenders and reading the proposals received side by side.

  4. 04

    Support through to completion

    Following conditions and discussions until the financing is in place, with your advisers.
