Independent partners

A clear collaboration with the professionals who bring us transactions.

A significant share of the transactions we review is presented to us by independent professionals: introducers, brokers, lawyers, accountants, wealth managers, family offices. This area is for them.

You know your client, the transaction and its constraints. We bring a lender's reading, the structuring of the file and the search for financing routes. The collaboration rests on defined roles and coordinated discussions, set out in writing before the first conversation with a lender.

You can present a transaction on behalf of a client from the transaction sheet, indicating your role. We come back to you, not to your client, unless you ask us otherwise. The terms of collaboration and remuneration are agreed in writing, case by case, before any step is taken.

Who we work with

Four partner profiles.

Introducers and independent brokers
You identify a transaction you do not wish to structure yourself, or one outside your usual scope. We take it on and keep you informed of its progress.
Lawyers and notaries
An acquisition, a restructuring or a succession calls for financing. We work in coordination with you, without encroaching on your advice.
Accountants and outsourced finance directors
You know your client's figures and repayment schedule. We turn that knowledge into a file a lender can read.
Wealth managers and family offices
Your clients hold assets through companies or investment vehicles. We handle the financing of those assets, never the management of their wealth.

How it works

Three stages, a written framework.

  1. 01

    You present the transaction

    From the transaction sheet, in a few minutes, indicating that you act as an introducer or adviser. Orders of magnitude are enough.

  2. 02

    We respond

    A first candid answer: the transaction seems reviewable to us, or not, and why. Then, if you wish, a three-way conversation with your client.

  3. 03

    We formalise the collaboration

    Each party's role, confidentiality, remuneration and information arrangements: everything is written down before the first presentation to a lender.

Our commitments

What you can expect from us.

  • Your client remains your client: we only deal with them with your agreement.
  • An answer to every transaction presented, even when it is negative.
  • Regular updates on progress, without you having to ask.
  • Written terms of collaboration agreed before any step is taken.
  • Confidentiality of the information provided, limited to the lenders consulted.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

01Do I need to send the full file from the outset?

No. The transaction sheet asks for orders of magnitude and a description. Documents come later, once we have confirmed that the transaction falls within our scope and you have decided to go further with us.

02How is the collaboration remunerated?

The terms are agreed in writing before any step is taken, case by case, in compliance with the rules applicable to your profession and to ours. We make no oral commitments and ask nothing of your client without your agreement.

03Can I present a transaction located outside the markets you serve?

Yes. We review transactions in the light of the asset, the structure and the relevant lenders, not of a map. We will tell you plainly if the country or the type of asset falls outside what we know how to do.

04What happens if the transaction does not go ahead?

We explain why, with the feedback of the lenders consulted where we are able to share it. That feedback often has value for what comes next: it shows what the file lacked or what should change in the structure.

A transaction to present on behalf of a client?

The transaction sheet takes a few minutes. Simply indicate your role: we come back to you.
