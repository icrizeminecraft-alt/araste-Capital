Transaction sheet

Present your transaction in detail.

The sheet gathers, in four short steps, what we need to give you a first view on feasibility. Orders of magnitude are enough; any optional field can be left blank.

What the sheet gives us

  • The transaction: type, purpose, amount, country, timeframe.
  • The asset: type, location, value and its basis, income, status.
  • The structure: borrowing entity, equity, existing debt, security.
  • The exit: how and when the financing will be repaid.

This form asks for no identity document, bank statement or financial document. Such elements will only be exchanged, where relevant, after a first contact and through an agreed channel.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What we are asked most often before a first conversation.

01When should you contact us?

As early as possible, as soon as a transaction takes shape: an acquisition to secure, a maturity approaching, a project to finance in stages. A first conversation allows us to understand where the need sits, identify the relevant documents and say whether we can help you, before the timetable tightens.

02What happens after the form is sent?

We review the details provided and come back to you through the channel you indicated. If the transaction falls within our scope, we arrange a conversation to understand it in detail and specify the documents to gather. Nothing is committed at this stage.

03What information should you prepare for a first conversation?

Orders of magnitude are enough: the type of need, the amount envisaged, the country of the transaction and the intended timeframe. Add a description of the asset or business, the structure and the intended exit. Detailed documents come later, if we decide together to go further.

04Do you work with private individuals?

No. We act for professional borrowers: companies, property companies, holding companies, investment vehicles, developers and professional owners. We do not deal with consumer credit or the financing of a main residence.

05How is the firm remunerated?

Remuneration arrangements are presented to you before any commitment and agreed with you in writing, ahead of the search for financing. No step is taken before that agreement.

06Do you guarantee that financing will be obtained?

The financing decision rests with the lender. Our commitment concerns the quality of the analysis and of the follow-up: the clarity of the file and the relevance of the routes explored. Our feedback is candid, including when it is negative.

07How is the information you provide handled?

It is used solely to review your request. It is neither used for marketing purposes nor sold or transferred to third parties. It is shared only with the lenders we consult for your transaction, with your agreement. The details are set out in the privacy policy.
